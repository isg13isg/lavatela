Complaints

If something went wrong (a machine that didn't work, an incorrect charge…), we want to know and fix it.

1. Send us your complaint

Fill in the form and we'll reply as soon as possible.

Prefer to talk? WhatsApp or +34 687 554 082. If you have a photo of the receipt or the charge, send it on WhatsApp.

2. Official complaint form

LAVA TELA has official Community of Madrid complaint forms available to customers. If you want to file one, ask us on WhatsApp or by phone and we'll give it to you.

  1. Fill in the form with your details, what happened and what you are asking for.
  2. One copy is for the business and one for you.
  3. If it isn't resolved within a reasonable time, submit your copy to the Municipal Consumer Information Office (OMIC) or to the Community of Madrid's Directorate-General for Consumer Affairs.
